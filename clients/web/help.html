Three Ways to Speak Commands

1. Hold to Talk (PTT)

Press and hold the large microphone button (or hold Spacebar) while speaking. Release when finished. Audio is sent immediately!

2. Auto-Detect (VAD)

Tap the mic once to enter listening mode. Speak naturally: the client automatically detects your voice and sends the audio after a brief pause (~0.8s silence).

3. Tap to Toggle

Manual two-tap mode: Tap once to start recording, speak your command at your own pace, and tap again to finish and submit.

Pro Tips & Best Practices

⚡ Pure Direct Signals

If you want to instantly test without speaking, use the Light ON and Light OFF quick action buttons. They send pure direct GPIO signals that bypass speech recognition completely.

📱 Mobile Wi-Fi Access

When accessing from an iPhone or Android phone across Wi-Fi, launch the server with --ssl (https://...) so the mobile browser permits microphone recording.

🎛️ Sensitivity Tuning

In noisy rooms, open the Settings panel in the header and slide Speech Sensitivity higher (e.g. 0.04 - 0.06) to filter out background television or ambient chatter.
